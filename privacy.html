Privacy Policy

RunSub is designed so training data serves your coaching, not advertising. This policy explains what data may be collected, how it is used, and the controls available to users.

Last updated: July 2026

1. What RunSub collects

RunSub may collect account details such as name, email address and app preferences. If you choose to connect training platforms or devices in future, RunSub may request activity, workout, pace, distance, heart rate, elevation, cadence, sleep, HRV, recovery and related training metrics where approved by the relevant platform and authorised by you.

2. How data is used

Training data is used to provide adaptive coaching, plan reviews, readiness context, goal confidence, workout recommendations, race preparation and progress insights. RunSub does not sell your personal training data.

3. Integrations

Platform integrations are under development and subject to partner approval. Where an integration is available, you will be asked to grant permission before RunSub accesses data from that service.

4. Data control

You may request access to your data, ask for corrections, disconnect integrations where supported, or request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting RunSub.

5. Retention and security

RunSub keeps data only for as long as needed to provide coaching, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and improve reliability. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used to protect stored data.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact hello@runsub.com.
